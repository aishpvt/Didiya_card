The part I wouldn’t say out loud

Ami jani amra erokom usually boli na, and toke bolte o ekhon amar ajob lagchhe. But majhe majhe seriously mone hoy, ekhon Didiya ke jiggesh korle pakka thik jinish ta ki korte hobe bole dito. Mane obhabei always hoye eshchhe amar, kichhu na janle eta jani je pakka Didiya jane amake bole debe.

You’re the person who remembers me when I was small, and a bit awful, and still figuring everything out. Nobody else has that. When you’re around I don’t have to explain who I used to be. Je ja i boluk na keno, Didiya always janto amake.

Shobar shob boka khawar por, and even tor shathe jhogra korar por, shei kadte kadte toh ami Didiya r kachhei jabo. Cause tor shathe marpit holeo shei amake toh tui i manate parbi. Jotoi jhamela hok na keno toke merechhi bole Ma Babu amake bolchhe, but still tui amar hoye bolbi Ma Babu ke je ar raag koro na or opor. Ab bolo, erokom Didiya ki shobar bhagge ashe?

I think we both know we’re not kids anymore. Someday this will all be stories we tell badly at each other’s kitchen tables. And sometimes I just feel sad je abar kobe will we be sharing the same roof?

happy 24th.
— Bonu
